# Supplier Corrosion - Inspection & Rework

**Real-World Quality Experience Case**  
*Industrial Manufacturing | Incoming Quality / Supplier Defect Handling*

> **Confidentiality:** This case study is anonymized. Company names, supplier names, internal identifiers, NCR numbers, specifications and other confidential information have been omitted.

## Overview

On October 5, 2026, during incoming quality activities, I worked with supplier components that had arrived with visible corrosion.

The affected parts included rings used for connection with a shaft.

Corrosion was observed in multiple areas, including:

- side surfaces,
- areas underneath the component,
- outer and inner surfaces,
- internal side areas,
- threaded areas.

## My Role

My responsibilities included:

- assessing the extent and location of corrosion,
- determining OK/NOK condition within my assigned responsibility,
- performing corrosion-removal rework on recoverable components,
- deciding the required rework approach based on the observed condition,
- and verifying the result after rework.

## Rework & Verification

For recoverable components, I removed the corrosion through the assigned rework process.

After rework, I checked the component step by step to verify that:

- visible corrosion had been removed,
- difficult areas had been inspected,
- no remaining unacceptable corrosion was present,
- and the corrosion did not return during the relevant observation period.

## Non-Recoverable Components

Not every component could be recovered.

At the time of this case, two components remained completely NOK because the corrosion had penetrated deeply enough into the metal that the parts had lost their intended usability.

This demonstrated the practical limit of rework: surface corrosion can be removed, but deeply compromised material cannot necessarily be restored through cleaning.
